# Remorrow — App Icon
Custom illustrated hourglass, 1024×1024 PNG at `Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon.png`.
The warm-gold hourglass reflects capturing moments before time passes. The dark ink background and minimal silhouette are intentionally distinct from generic sparkles and blue AI gradients.

The image is intended as a first original direction rather than a trademark or App Store approval claim. The asset catalog is linked in `project.yml`; regenerate the Xcode project after pulling and inspect the icon on an actual Home Screen.
