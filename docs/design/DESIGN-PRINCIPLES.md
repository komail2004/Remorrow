# Product Design Principles
- Premium, gentle, minimal aesthetic; warm language and clear hierarchy.
- Core flow: capture -> understand -> review -> save, without unnecessary onboarding.
- Never hide uncertainty; make dates, timezone and destinations easy to correct.
- Use native iOS controls, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver and reduced-motion support.
- Clear empty/error/permission states. No dark patterns around the $4.99 upfront purchase.
- Make shopping-return tracking feel practical rather than promising a merchant policy guarantee.
