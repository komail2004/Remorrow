# Go-to-market hypothesis
**Positioning:** "Share it. Remember it." A polished one-time-purchase alternative to manual date transcription.

## Pricing
$4.99 one-time iOS upfront is a hypothesis, not a guaranteed conversion rate. Model Apple commissions, refunds, taxes and ad acquisition cost separately. Consider a launch price experiment if supported.

## Discovery
- App Store subtitle should communicate "Text to Reminders & Calendar" without overclaiming AI capabilities.
- Focus on high-intent use cases: text-to-calendar, screenshot reminder, return deadline tracker.
- Demo a real 10-second workflow; short organic videos and small creator outreach.
- Small capped Apple Ads tests only after product conversion is measurable; stop unprofitable terms.

## Validation
Conduct 30–50 usability tests with real sanitized content, review store search competition, use actual paid downloads and acquisition cost rather than views or guesses. Do not promise $20K revenue.
