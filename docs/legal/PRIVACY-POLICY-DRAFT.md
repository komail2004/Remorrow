# Remorrow — Privacy Policy Draft (November 2026)

**Status: draft for owner and legal review. Not yet a published App Store privacy-policy URL.**

## What we process
Remorrow processes text that you type, paste or explicitly share; screenshots, document photos and receipts that you select or capture; dates and titles you approve; and return/subscription details you choose to save. It does not automatically read SMS, Messages, email, contacts or your photo library.

## Where processing occurs
Text extraction from photos uses Apple Vision on the device. The default action extraction uses local rules. Where supported and enabled, Apple's Foundation Models may interpret selected content on the device. This app currently has no developer-operated server, login system, remote analytics SDK or advertising SDK to receive selected content.

## Data storage
Remorrow stores your approved plans, selected source text, original images, receipts, subscriptions and preferences in local app storage. Temporary shares from other apps are delivered through an iOS App Group inbox; deliberately provided text from an App Shortcut uses an app-private inbox. Saved items remain until deleted. iOS device backups may include this app's local data depending on your backup settings; this must be described accurately in the final hosted policy.

## Calendar, Reminders and notifications
With your permission, Remorrow can use Apple EventKit to check the free/busy state of calendars visible to the app, and to create or edit events and reminders only after approval. Calendar and Reminders services may independently sync data using accounts you enabled in iOS, such as iCloud or a third-party calendar provider. Local notifications for receipts, warranties or subscriptions use generic lock-screen text. Widget details are opt-in.

## Your choices
You may edit suggestions, decline Calendar/Reminders access, disable notifications, avoid attaching source images, and delete saved items in the app. When a linked plan is deleted, the app attempts to remove the corresponding Apple event or reminder. Check the system app if an error occurs.

## Sharing and sale
The present Remorrow code does not send saved content to a developer server, does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not sell personal data. Apple's system services operate under their own policies.

## Contact, dates and final review
**Before launch, the owner must add a genuine support/privacy contact and publicly reachable policy URL, review backup/retention details and Apple's current App Store privacy disclosures, and confirm this draft still matches the shipped binary and connected services.**
